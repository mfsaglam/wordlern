WordLern

The 1000 German words that do the work.

A few thousand words make up a language. A thousand of them make up most of what anyone actually says. WordLern teaches those, one card at a time, and schedules the review for you.

What it does

  • A word list that was edited, not scraped

    1000 lemmas ranked by frequency in the Leipzig Corpora Collection, each given a single English meaning and one A1-level German example sentence — all checked by hand, one sense per word.

  • The Leitner system

    Cards move through five boxes. Answer correctly and a card waits longer before it comes back; miss it and it returns to box one tomorrow. You study the words you keep forgetting, not the ones you know.

  • Spoken German

    Every word and every sentence can be read aloud using the best German voice installed on your device.

  • Progress you can see

    Words mastered out of 1000, the five boxes side by side, and a recap at the end of every session showing what moved.

  • Widgets

    The same progress on your home screen and lock screen, with the number of cards due — or when the next one is.

  • One reminder, at the right time

    A single notification scheduled for the moment your next card is actually due. Not a daily nag at nine o'clock.

No account. No ads. No subscription. No tracking.

WordLern makes no network calls at all. Everything you do stays on your device, which also means it works on a plane, on a train, and in a basement.
